# Assignment 1 — Grading Rubric

**Bong Study Hub — Foundation Batch 2026 · Class 01: Welcome to Computer Science**

**Total: 10 points.** This is a beginner assignment. Grade for thinking,
not polish.

## What This Rubric Does NOT Grade

- ❌ Handwriting quality
- ❌ English fluency (beyond being understandable)
- ❌ Programming knowledge (none is expected — no code should appear at all)
- ❌ Complexity — a simple, correct, precise answer beats an elaborate,
  confusing one. Do not reward unnecessarily complicated answers.

---

## Scoring Table

| # | Dimension | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Correct Input identification | 2 |
| 2 | Correct Process identification | 2 |
| 3 | Correct Output identification | 2 |
| 4 | Algorithm clarity and sequence | 2 |
| 5 | Precision / completeness | 2 |
| | **Total** | **10** |

---

## Dimension 1 — Correct Input Identification (2 points)

**2 points — Excellent:** All the information/materials genuinely needed
before the process can start are identified, with nothing essential
missing.

**1 point — Good:** Most inputs are identified; one or two minor items
are missing, or one listed item is actually part of the process rather
than an input.

**0 points — Needs Improvement:** Input is missing, incorrect, or
confused with the process or output.

## Dimension 2 — Correct Process Identification (2 points)

**2 points — Excellent:** The process description accurately captures
what actually happens, in the right general order, without needing the
full algorithm to make sense.

**1 point — Good:** The process is mostly accurate but vague in places,
or a step is out of place.

**0 points — Needs Improvement:** Process is missing, wrong, or is
really just a restatement of the input or output.

## Dimension 3 — Correct Output Identification (2 points)

**2 points — Excellent:** The output clearly and correctly states the
end result of the process.

**1 point — Good:** An output is given but is vague (e.g., "it's done")
rather than specific (e.g., "order placed successfully").

**0 points — Needs Improvement:** Output is missing or incorrect (e.g.,
describes an input or a mid-process action instead).

## Dimension 4 — Algorithm Clarity and Sequence (2 points)

**2 points — Excellent:** Steps are logical, ordered, and understandable
— a stranger could follow them without guessing.

**1 point — Good:** Mostly logical but some steps are unclear, out of
order, or a small gap would need to be inferred.

**0 points — Needs Improvement:** Steps are missing, out of order, or do
not form a usable sequence at all.

## Dimension 5 — Precision / Completeness (2 points)

**2 points — Excellent:** At least 5 steps, each describing one clear
action; no significant assumption is silently left unstated (the "no gas
available" kind of gap from the tea activity is avoided).

**1 point — Good:** Meets the 5-step minimum, but one or two steps bundle
multiple actions together, or leave a detail a computer would need
unstated.

**0 points — Needs Improvement:** Fewer than 5 steps, or steps are so
vague that a computer (or a stranger) genuinely could not act on them.

---

## Optional Challenge — Not Scored in the 10 Points

The Optional Challenge (decisions, failure case, assumption, edge case)
is **not** part of the required 10 points and must never be needed to
reach full marks. If a student attempts it well, acknowledge it warmly in
your written feedback (see `04_Feedback_Comments.md`) — a small bonus
note or verbal recognition is appropriate, but do not build it into the
numeric score. Beginners who skip it should never see their grade
affected.

---

## Quick Reference — What "Full Marks" Looks Like

A 10/10 submission:
- Names a real, specific input, process, and output for one chosen
  everyday process.
- Gives 5+ steps, each one clear action, in the order they actually
  happen.
- Could be handed to someone unfamiliar with the process and followed
  without them having to guess anything important.
- Contains no code.

That's the whole bar. Nothing more is required for full marks.
